Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take for a customized base?

Because each customized base is 3D printed to order, please allow 3-5 business days for production, followed by standard shipping times. We'll send tracking information as soon as it ships.

Can I order extra tokens?

Absolutely! The base kit comes with 30 tokens, but we sell "Refill Packs" of 30 tokens. If your students are participating so much you run out, we count that as a massive win!

Do you accept purchase orders (POs) from schools?

Yes, we gladly accept formal Purchase Orders from educational institutions and districts. Please contact our support team to set up an invoice.

How durable is the 3D printed plastic?

Very durable. We use a high-density infill setting and thick walls to ensure the LemLearn tower can easily survive the rigors of daily classroom life, including accidental knocks off a desk.
